# Photographs

All images are from [picsum.photos](https://picsum.photos), which serves
photographs from Unsplash under the
[Unsplash licence](https://unsplash.com/license) — free to use, no attribution
required. They are committed here rather than fetched at render time so the
site initializer works on a machine with no network, which is the situation a
conference demo is most likely to be in.

They were chosen by eye from contact sheets of 700 candidates: picsum has no
subject search, so there is no query that would reproduce this selection.
